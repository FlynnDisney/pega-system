# PEGA Email Automation Schedule

## Overview

Users receive **14 total emails** over 4 weeks:
- 1 Welcome email (Day 0)
- 12 Emotion-focused emails (Days 0-19)
- 1 Reassessment email (Day 28)

All emails focus on the user's THREE LOWEST scoring emotions.

## Email Schedule Pattern

### Emotion 1 (Primary Deficit - Lowest Score)
- **Day 0**: Understanding [Emotion]
- **Day 1**: First Intervention
- **Day 3**: Second Intervention
- **Day 5**: Integration

### Emotion 2 (Secondary Deficit - Second Lowest)
- **Day 7**: Understanding [Emotion]
- **Day 8**: First Intervention
- **Day 10**: Second Intervention
- **Day 12**: Integration

### Emotion 3 (Tertiary Deficit - Third Lowest)
- **Day 14**: Understanding [Emotion]
- **Day 15**: First Intervention
- **Day 17**: Second Intervention
- **Day 19**: Integration

## Welcome Email

Sent immediately upon assessment completion (Day 0, before Emotion 1).

## Email Content Structure

Each emotion sequence follows this pattern:

1. **Email 1 - Understanding**: Theory and conditions (~150 words)
2. **Email 2 - First Intervention**: Practical exercise with steps (~180 words)
3. **Email 3 - Second Intervention**: Building on first exercise (~180 words)
4. **Email 4 - Integration**: Summary and continuation (~150 words)

## Footer

All emails include this promotional footer:

> Are you a coach, clinician or teacher interested in the power of positive emotions? Can you imagine your clients or students with [emotion-specific benefits]? Join the Joy Study Teacher Training for six months of science-based, peer-supported education based on direct experience and personal feedback. Find more information at www.flynndisney.com/joy

## Total Timeline

- **Day 0**: Welcome email + Emotion 1 Email 1
- **Days 1-5**: Emotion 1 sequence (Emails 2-4)
- **Day 6**: Rest day
- **Days 7-12**: Emotion 2 sequence
- **Day 13**: Rest day
- **Days 14-19**: Emotion 3 sequence
- **Days 20-27**: Reflection period
- **Day 28**: Reassessment email

Total: 28 days (4 weeks)

## Reassessment Email (Day 28)

**Subject:** Time to measure your positive emotion growth

**Purpose:** Invite users to retake the PEGA assessment to measure their progress

**Content includes:**
- Reminder of their 3 focus emotions
- Link to reassessment
- Explanation that results will show before/after comparison
- Encouragement about emotion development
- CTA for Joy Study Teacher Training

**Results Display:**
- Original scores (from initial assessment)
- Current scores (from reassessment)
- Change/gain for each emotion
- Visual comparison chart
